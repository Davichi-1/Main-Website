About

Hi, I’m David.

I’m David Chidera Tobias, a creative based in Nigeria. I work under the name Davichi Concept: videos, designs and websites.

I began with graphic design and kept adding skills until a project could come to me as an idea and leave finished. I’m studying B.Sc. Computer Science at University of the People and DevOps at Livingworth Academy, and I keep learning by building and contributing to open source as Davichi-1 on GitHub.

Right now I’m the sound engineer and lead graphic designer at RCCG Praise & Power Zonal Headquarters, running live sound and the branding for its programs.
